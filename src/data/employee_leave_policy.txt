Employee Leave Policy

1. Annual Leave
Employees receive 20 days of annual leave per calendar year.

2. Sick Leave
Employees may request sick leave according to company policy and applicable regulations.

3. Leave Requests
Employees should submit planned leave requests to their reporting manager at least five working days in advance.

4. Approval
Leave requests require manager approval before the leave begins, except where emergency procedures apply.

5. Questions
Employees should contact the Human Resources department for clarification about leave eligibility or policy interpretation.
